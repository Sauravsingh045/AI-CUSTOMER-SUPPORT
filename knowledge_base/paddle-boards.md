# Paddle boards

## Product details
Choose a board by the rider's weight, including gear:
- 10'6" All-round: up to 85 kg; suited to beginners and calm water.
- 11' All-round: up to 110 kg; the most stable option and suitable for two light riders.
- 12'6" Touring: up to 130 kg; longer and faster, intended for trips and experienced paddlers.
Choose the larger board when the rider is between sizes.

## Included items
Each board includes the board, an adjustable 3-piece paddle, a double-action hand pump with gauge, a removable center fin, a coiled leash, a valve wrench, and a carry bag.
Part numbers: fin PB-FIN, pump PB-PUMP, paddle PB-PADDLE, leash PB-LEASH, valve wrench PB-WRENCH, carry bag PB-BAG.

## Setup and use
Inflate the board to 12-15 PSI. Below 12 PSI it may bend in the middle. A pressure drop of 1-2 PSI overnight can happen as the air cools and does not by itself indicate a leak.

## Troubleshooting
For a slow leak, check the valve core first:
1. Inflate the board to 15 PSI.
2. Apply soapy water to the valve. Bubbles indicate escaping air.
3. Use the valve wrench to turn the valve core a quarter-turn clockwise.
4. Apply soapy water again and check the pressure after 24 hours.

If it still loses more than 2 PSI in 24 hours, collect the order number, board size, and how many days ago it arrived. A seam split must be handled by a colleague.

## Parts and replacements
A colleague arranges all shipments; do not promise one in a reply. A missing accessory is shipped without requiring a return. For a leak that continues after the valve check, a new board is provided within 30 days of delivery; after 30 days, a patch kit is provided. A split seam is escalated to a colleague.

To arrange help, collect the order number, board size, and a short description of the problem.
