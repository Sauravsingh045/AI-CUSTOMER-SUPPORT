# Returns and warranty

## Returns
Start returns through Your Orders on Amazon within 30 days of delivery. Amazon provides the return label and handles the refund. We cannot start, expedite, or approve a return or refund; a colleague handles refund questions.

## Warranty
The warranty covers manufacturing faults:
- Paddle boards: 2 years for the board, seams, and valve; 1 year for the paddle, pump, fin, and leash.
- Shelving: 2 years for panels and hardware.

Damage from use, such as a puncture caused by a rock, is not covered.

## Other products
For other Harbor & Pine products, returns go through Amazon within 30 days of delivery, and manufacturing faults have a 2-year warranty.
