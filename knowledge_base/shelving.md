# Shelving

## Models and finishes
All four flat-pack models come in natural oak, white, black, and walnut:
- HP-S3: 3-shelf bookcase, 106 cm high.
- HP-S5: 5-shelf bookcase, 176 cm high.
- HP-C4: 2x2 cube unit, 77 cm high.
- HP-C9: 3x3 cube unit, 112 cm high.
The model code is on the assembly manual and box label.

## Parts
Each panel's number sticker combines with the model code to form its part number (for example, HP-S3-04).
- HP-S3: 1 left side, 2 right side, 3 top, 4 shelf, 5 bottom, 6 back panel.
- HP-S5: 1 left side, 2 right side, 3 top, 4-6 shelves, 7 bottom, 8 back panel, 9 kick plate.
- HP-C4: 1 top, 2 bottom, 3 left side, 4 right side, 5 center divider, 6 shelf, 7 back panel.
- HP-C9: 1 top, 2 bottom, 3 left side, 4 right side, 5-6 dividers, 7-8 shelves, 9 back panel.
Hardware kits HK-S and HK-C are for the S and C models respectively. Each kit contains the cam locks, dowels, screws, and wall anchor.

## Assembly
Lay panels on the box or a rug to avoid scratches. For cam locks, point the arrow toward the cam-bolt hole and turn clockwise about half a turn until it stops. Install the back panel with its finished side facing into the shelf.

Secure the tall HP-S5 and HP-C9 units to the wall with the included anchor. Assembly takes about 30-60 minutes for one person; a cordless screwdriver on a low setting is suitable.

## Damage and missing items
A colleague arranges all shipments; do not promise one in a reply. Missing screws or hardware are handled with the model's hardware kit. For one or two damaged panels, the affected panels are shipped by part number. Three or more damaged panels or a damaged frame qualify for a full replacement. A wrong finish is exchanged for the correct finish.

Collect the order number, model, and damaged panel part numbers. A photo helps.
