# Shipping

## Orders
Amazon ships all orders. Customers can find delivery dates and tracking in Your Orders on Amazon. We cannot view or change an Amazon delivery.

## Replacement shipments
Replacement parts and kits ship free from our warehouse. They leave within 2 business days and arrive within 3-7 business days. A tracking link is emailed when the parcel leaves. A colleague arranges every shipment; do not promise one in a reply.

## Service area
Products are sold in the countries where they are listed on Amazon. Replacement parts go to the same address as the original order.
